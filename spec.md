# Walk in Truth — governing spec

Edit this file when the teaching changes. Then tell Grok (this chat or Grok Build) to apply `spec.md` to `engine.js`. Nothing updates the engine by itself.

## Measure
Truth is in Scripture and a living walk with God. Feeling, old experience, and psychology are not the judge.

## Walk
1. You first — how you hear, what you have not tested, whether you receive that God loves you.
2. Who — marriage (covenant) or dating (not that promise).
3. What they actually said — words, not your story.
4. How to walk now — one next step from the engine.

## Spiritual
- An untested thought, old story, or past fact glued to a dark feeling can become a hold. Name it or you cannot deal with it. Take it captive (2 Corinthians 10:4-5).
- God loves them. Many take a long time to receive that. Experience must not sit above His Word and His nature.
- Pain, fear, rejection, betrayal can make it hard to look. Ask God for courage to examine one thought. Do not leave the lie standing.
- Invite God in. Choose Him. Declare the Word. Receive and give forgiveness. Receive and declare healing.
- Spouse is not the enemy. The thief steals; Jesus gives life.

## Covenant
- Wife obeys as unto the Lord, not because he is better.
- She may challenge him toward godliness with a quiet, noble spirit — not to win.
- Husband leads, protects, guides; kind and gentle. He is not her saviour.
- Neither may lead the other into sin or away from Christ.
- Forgiveness and leading-into-sin are two different things. Forgiveness is required for healing and for the relationship.
- Not a battle of the sexes. Submit yourself to God first.
- Dating is not marriage.

## Do not
- Do not start from abuse or “if it is safe.”
- Do not diagnose.
- Do not dump a warfare lecture on every screen. Keep that in the reasoning engine.
