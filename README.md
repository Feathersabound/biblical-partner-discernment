# Walk in Truth

A browser app for ongoing relationship and communication practice under Scripture.

Open `index.html`. No server. Records stay on the device (localStorage).

## Modes

- **Home** — 14-day picture: asked, blessed, quickly provoked
- **Incident** — full discernment walk for one event
- **Check-in** — daily speech, asking, anger, surrender
- **Practice** — six repeatable acts that form the walk
- **History** — what you saved on this device

## Direction

Not a one-off test. A loop: name the fact, test the belief, clean the speech, name the trigger, surrender to the Lord, do it again. Healing is movement. Destructive loops are named, not protected.

Doctrine is unchanged: marriage covenant, dating is not, wife obeys as unto the Lord, husband must not abuse or command sin, 1 Corinthians 13, words as blessing or curse.
